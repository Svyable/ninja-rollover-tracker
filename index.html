🩹 Injury Overlay — the compounding problem, and how to stop it

What just happened isn't just bad luck — it's structure. One running back's Q1 exit threatens three legs at once because the SGP stacked them in a single game, on a single backfield. Here's the playbook:
  1. Cap same-game exposure at 2 legs. The SGP put 3 legs in PHI@TEN — one injury, one blowout benching, one weather delay, and a third of the ticket shares a fate. Spread the risk across games.
  2. Never let two legs depend on the same player's health. Saquon's TD leg is Saquon's body. If a leg's entire thesis is one man's ligaments, that's a single point of failure wearing a jersey.
  3. Spread across time slots. Early + late games give you live information and a chance to hedge or cash out. Ten legs all kicking off at 1pm is ten simultaneous coin flips with no escape hatch.
  4. Balance player props with team-level bets. Spreads and totals survive a single injury; player props don't. A ticket that's 100% "star does star thing" is a portfolio of knees.
  5. Read the practice report. Limited or DNP midweek = downgrade the model on the spot, or fade the leg entirely. The report is free; the lesson costs $100.
  6. Respect the math of many legs. If each leg carries even a ~3% in-game injury risk, 10 legs means roughly a 1-in-4 chance somebody gets hurt. Same-game stacks don't diversify that — they concentrate it.
  7. When a leg dies early, check your protection first. FanDuel's Bet Protect+ voids injured-player legs instead of grading them losses — which is exactly what's saving this ticket right now. No protection on the ticket? Then it's dead: take any cash-out the app insults you with and go touch grass.
  8. Opt into injury protection whenever it's offered. A void turns a 0% leg into a repriced, still-live ticket. It is the single highest-EV checkbox in parlay betting — and today it's the only reason we're still watching.
🔬 The asymmetry edge: injuries don't hurt every leg equally — they help unders. If a QB goes down, the under on his passing yards gets safer; if a WR limps off, the opposing defense's job gets easier. Pair fragile overs with antifragile unders and the injury gods start working for you. (Ward under 185.5 says thanks.)

How a leg gets graded

implied p = risk ÷ (risk + win)  →  −270 ⇒ 270/370 = 73.0%
edge = model p − implied p  →  +2pp means the price is 2 points soft
EV per $100 = model p × payout − 100
bust p = 1 − model p  →  the Kill Order ranking

Ticket probability is the product of leg probabilities (legs treated as independent — the SGP trio is decomposed into estimated standalone prices that multiply to ~18.4%, matching the +447 bundle's 18.28% implied, so the book's correlation handling is visible in the open).
